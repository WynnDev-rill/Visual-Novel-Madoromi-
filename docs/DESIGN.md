# Madoromi — design decisions, revised 4 November 2026

A complete intimate school romance adapted from the user's short story, with Indonesian prose, Japanese setting, three acts, an earned confession and a playable afterstory. Choices change the way the pair open up and later callbacks, without punitive affection scores. The original ending is preserved.

Ren'Py 8.5.3 is the selected baseline: native save/load, rollback, history, accessibility and Android packaging leave production effort for art, staging, music and prose. Installed Godot 4.6.3 is the comparison candidate, but would require VN-system integration for no requested gameplay advantage. The toolchain must prove this choice with actual builds.

Visual direction revised from the user's twelve ATRI / Please Be Happy references: mid-September light, azure/white system surfaces, navy/slate ink, a world-dominant title, open outlined ADV dialogue over a restrained reading shade, and a compact navy touch toolbar. The latest user revision prioritizes the supplied ATRI system, save and backlog examples; the original cream PBH balloon treatment is superseded. System screens use horizontal navigation and segmented preferences; choices stay below faces. The chosen Drive icon is app identity, not the heroine design.

## Cast, world and acting

The canonical cast comes from the user's four `*_base_bust_v1.png` concepts supplied through Google Drive on 4 November. These are cropped/upscaled upper-body design concepts from an AI lineup, not pre-existing full-body sprites or a Live2D rig. Production extends the supplied designs into consistent standing portraits, expressions and costumes. Aoi keeps blonde hair gathered into a low side bun, two narrow dark hairpins, thin round glasses, amber eyes, an ivory vest and a navy/gold uniform. Mio keeps blue-black hair and glasses; Sora keeps dark hair, glasses and headphones; Ren keeps ash-brown hair and his calm first-person identity.

Acting must convey more than changing mouths or eyebrows. Aoi's shy clasped hands, open laugh and hand-at-heart confession poses are integrated at specific emotional beats. Mio's thoughtful glasses gesture and Sora's relaxed headphone/pocket posture support their practical roles. Neutral poses leave room for the text. Short native dissolves connect meaningful pose changes; framing can move closer when the relationship changes. No forced Live2D, exaggerated idle bounce, or constant portrait movement. Reduced motion remains available.

All environments are unified with the cast's fine-line, softly painted 2D finish: muted foliage, navy/slate shadows, warm wood and ivory light in one fictional coastal town. The ten scene backgrounds cover day/night bedroom, cafeteria, library beanbag corner, day/night shopping street, bakery, chapel/tree courtyard rehearsal stage and school music room. Day/night variants retain their room or street layout. The handmade jeans prop belongs to this same visual family: women’s high-rise relaxed-straight ankle jeans, small painted motifs, and a matching casual epilogue sprite. Old third-party baseline sprites and rendered backgrounds are historical curation, not a second style to mix into the final game. Production provenance and source/output hashes belong in `docs/licenses/production-art.json`; the earlier audit remains in the license evidence directory.

Reading text and interface labels use bundled Noto Sans. Noto Serif Italic remains a deliberate subtitle accent. Colors: ink `#153d5a`, white `#f3faff`, sea `#2086b5`, azure `#47b6dc`, outline `#79cde5`. System screens use a coastal header, thin borders and large tabs; backlog uses a navy overlay with white text. See [ATRI_UI_REVISION.md](ATRI_UI_REVISION.md). Default dialogue sizes 36 / 42 virtual pixels on a 1920×1080 reference, assessed in real scaled Android screenshots. Primary touch controls are at least 108 virtual pixels tall before device scaling. On the tested 1280×720, 240 dpi Android emulator this renders 72 physical pixels, or 48 dp. Other densities still require device-specific checks; virtual coordinates are not universally Android dp.

Desktop and Android share a 1920×1080 design with scalable touch controls. Native engine features cover standard VN systems. Custom work is limited to Madoromi's title/menu, readable dialogue, chapter navigation, phone scene presentation and art direction. Preferences include motion reduction, font size, text/auto speed and audio channels.

The complete script has received a targeted editorial pass using haowjy's `writing-principles`, `creative-writing-craft`, `story-review` and `story-memory`. Its source ending, five choices, callbacks and twelve-scene structure remain intact. [STORY_AUDIT.md](STORY_AUDIT.md) records the actual revisions. Audio remains four licensed Kevin MacLeod instrumental tracks plus ambience and soft effects, with no character voice acting and no benchmark-game music.

## Acceptance
Complete story; choices and callbacks; no missing assets; readable real screenshots at desktop/mobile sizes; native persistence and history work; audio legal and mixed; final APK exists, is signed, has the provided icon, and passes available installation/launch checks. Physical-device testing is reported honestly if unavailable. Aspirational Steam quality is not a ranking claim.

Motion uses native ATL: a 320ms restrained portrait entrance, 200ms exit, 220ms expression/pose dissolve, and slower scene fades/camera drift for changes of place. Routine UI transitions remain short; reduced motion removes portrait displacement and continuous scene drift. No spring/bounce or portrait deformation is used.

Android audio selects SDL’s native Java AudioTrack backend before the device opens. This retains all music/ambience/SFX controls while avoiding the OpenSLES buffer-mutex stall observed on the API21 software test device. Final APK verification must run without the diagnostic emulator wrap override.
