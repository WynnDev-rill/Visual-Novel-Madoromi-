# Coherent MADOROMI environments and gift prop

Production direction revised 4 November 2026. The initial asset-first pass used mature licensed backgrounds and generated a few story-specific gaps. After the user supplied the canonical cast and requested a consistent whole-game style, the environment pass was expanded to one fine-line painted 2D family for every story location.

The style reference is the supplied character concept and its approved production expansion: delicate anime contours, softly painted matte surfaces, muted foliage, navy/slate shadows, ivory highlights and warm wood. Kazehama is one fictional Japanese coastal town in mid-September. Reference-game screenshots guide interface and composition decisions; their artwork is not copied into these images.

The painted-jeans gift prop uses a women's high-rise relaxed-straight, ankle-length cut suitable for Aoi's Japanese everyday wardrobe. The unfolded insert shows its shape; the casual sprite matches the denim, waist, pocket and hip motifs. Functional text research from UNIQLO Japan and individual denim artist Kessler Ramirez informed cut and fine-brush treatment. No external fashion photograph or artwork was submitted to generation or copied. Exact sources and lineage are in `production-art-provenance.json`. Its small ant, bread, cloud, fish, station and wave motifs come from the story. The gift is revealed only after Aoi accepts Ren's confession and their relationship is made explicit.

The courtyard and daytime bedroom proofs were visually reviewed before expanding the set. All ten environments, day/night continuity, character compositing and the prop have received visual inspection; actual engine screenshots are recorded in the verification report. The production manifest `docs/licenses/production-art.json` records all 33 selected runtime images and their transformations. This art register does not establish APK runtime compatibility.

Images are generated through OpenAI image generation for this project. Native generation dimensions are recorded rather than calling resized images native 1920×1080. Approved runtime WebPs are committed under `game/images/originals/`; generated originals remain in the task workspace. No exclusive-copyright or human-illustration claim is made. User-supplied character concepts are authorized design inputs; private original-reference JPGs and benchmark-game art are not generation inputs or distributed source files.

The earlier 3 November courtyard, bakery, lounge and prop were a baseline gap-filling pass. They are superseded by the coherent production selection rather than mixed into it. Historical third-party background acquisition evidence remains in the license register.
