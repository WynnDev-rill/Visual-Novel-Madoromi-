# Original environment gap assets

The reusable Potat0Master packs were inspected first. They cover school, bedroom, town, cafe, nature and rehearsal locations, but not the source story's cathedral/tree, bakery bread display, or beanbag art-room corner.

Three environment-only images were generated on 2026-11-03 with OpenAI image generation for this project, then resized to 1920×1080 WebP for runtime. No third-party sprite or benchmark image was submitted to generation. Generated originals remain in the task workspace; runtime images are committed under `game/images/originals/`.

- `courtyard.webp`: fictional coastal Japanese cathedral/tree courtyard, noon in September.
- `bakery.webp`: neighborhood bakery, bread counter and quiet street, noon.
- `lounge.webp`: school art-room corner, paired beanbags, pencils and paper.
- `jeans.webp`: original gift prop illustration, denim with small motifs from the story, made after reusable prop search could not cover this specific handmade gift.

These are AI-generated project assets; no claim of exclusive copyright or human illustration is made. They do not reproduce ATRI artwork. This small gap pipeline supplements the reused character and environment families.
